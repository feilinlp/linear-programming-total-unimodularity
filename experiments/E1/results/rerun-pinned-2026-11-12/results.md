## Validation

- Both solvers proved optimality for every recorded instance.
- Every LP has an independently checked, exact rational primal/dual certificate.
- Every non-bipartite instance has a verified odd cycle with determinant magnitude 2.
- All simplex coordinates are in {0, 1/2, 1}; every ILP vector is a valid 0/1 cover.
- All analytical control optima agree; all instances with n <= 12 agree with exhaustive cover enumeration.
- Every threshold-rounded LP vector is a cover of size at most 2 * LP.

## Timing scope

Per-instance times are the median of 1 call(s). Sparse matrix construction is excluded; SciPy/HiGHS conversion, presolve (ILP only), solving and postsolve are included. These are diagnostic timings, not a controlled LP-versus-ILP speed benchmark.

## Interpretation

An exact non-TU example refutes the claim that TU is necessary for one objective and right-hand side. It does not refute the all-integer-right-hand-sides characterisation of TU. A fractional returned answer can occur even when an integral optimum exists. An integer-valued LP objective also need not imply exactness (two disjoint triangles).
